System Name: Alkar UAV

Mission: An unmanned aerial vehicle developed to provide reconnaissance, surveillance, and rapid situational awareness.

Configuration: Quadcopter (X Configuration)

Number of Motors: 4

Propeller Size: 7 inch
 
Target Flight Time: 20-30 minutes

Maximum Takeoff Weight (MTOW): ~ 2-2.5 kg

Payload: ~ 0.5–0.8 kg (Camera, sensors, or testing equipment)

Operating Environment: Outdoor

Primary Design Objective: A CNC-machinable motor mount designed to securely attach to a 25x25 mm carbon fiber arm, engineered to provide maximum structural rigidity while keeping the weight to an absolute minimum.
