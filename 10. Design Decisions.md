# Design Decisions

| Observation | Estimated Reason | Confidence Level |
|---|---|---|
| Fillets used | To reduce stress concentration | 90% |
| 4 bolts | To better handle vibration and moment | 60% |
| Clamp connection | To avoid drilling the carbon fiber and ease of maintenance | 50% |
| Motor aligned with the arm axis | The motor generates torque, thus forcing the attached arm to rotate around its own axis. If it is not on the arm axis, being to the right or left can cause damage to the arms. Being above or below can create the same effect. M = F × e. Here, e is the eccentricity. If the motor moves away from the axis, an additional bending moment is generated on the arm. Therefore, not only the motor torque but also the moment created by the thrust affects the design. | 60% |
| Pocketing (pocket milling) | To reduce weight | 50% |
| Small geometry | To reduce weight | 50% |
