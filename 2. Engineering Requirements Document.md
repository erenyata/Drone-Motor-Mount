# Engineering Requirements Document

## 1. Functional Requirements
FR-001: The motor mount shall ensure the secure attachment of the motor to the aircraft.
FR-002: The motor mount shall maintain the position of the motor under design loads and prevent unwanted displacements.

## 2. Interface Requirements
IR-001: The motor mount shall be compatible with a 25x25 mm carbon fiber arm.
IR-002: The motor mount shall be compatible with the mounting geometry of the selected motor.

## 3. Performance Requirements
PR-001: The motor mount shall maintain its structural integrity under the defined design loads.
PR-002: The mass of the motor mount shall not exceed TBD grams.
 
## 4. Manufacturing Requirements
MR-001: The motor mount shall be manufacturable using a 3-axis CNC milling process.

## 5. Verification Requirements
VR-001: All critical dimensions shall be verified via the CAD model.
VR-002: Structural strength shall be verified through Finite Element Analysis (FEA).
VR-003: The part mass shall be verified by calculation via the CAD model.
