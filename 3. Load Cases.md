# Load Cases

## LC-001 Hover
The drone is hovering at a constant altitude (steady hover).
The motors produce sufficient thrust to balance the weight.

## LC-002 Maximum Thrust
The motor produces maximum continuous thrust.
This represents one of the highest axial loads on the motor mount.

## LC-003 Maximum Torque
The motor produces maximum torque.
The reaction torque generated on the motor body must be counteracted by the motor mount.

## LC-004 3g Maneuver
The drone performs an aggressive maneuver at approximately 3g.
Inertial forces resulting from the motor mass impose additional loads on the motor mount.
