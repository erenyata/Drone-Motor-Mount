# Material Selection

## Candidate Materials

- Aluminum 6061-T6
- Aluminum 7075-T6
- Aluminum 2024-T3
- Ti-6Al-4V
- Carbon Fiber Composite (CFRP)

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|-------:|
| Specific Strength | 30% |
| Stiffness | 20% |
| Fatigue Resistance | 20% |
| Machinability | 15% |
| Corrosion Resistance | 10% |
| Cost | 5% |

## Material Assesment 

### CFRP
* Anisotropic material.
* Damage can occur around the holes.
* The manufacturing process is more complex.
* Does not fully comply with MR-001 and MR-004.

### Titanyum
* Very expensive.
* Difficult to machine via CNC.
* Our loads are quite low.

#### 2024-T3
* Low corrosion resistance.

### 6061-T6
#### Pros
* Better corrosion resistance
* Lower cost
* Easier machinability

#### Cons
* Lower strength
* Requires larger cross-sections

### 7075-T6
#### Pros
* Higher strength
* Lower weight
* Smaller geometry

#### Eksileri
* Lower corrosion resistance
* Higher cost

The 7075-T6 alloy was selected due to its high specific strength, adequate stiffness, good machinability, and compatibility with benchmark studies. By providing higher strength compared to the 6061-T6 alloy, it will make it possible to achieve a lighter and more compact geometry. Its corrosion resistance against outdoor conditions can be enhanced by applying an anodized coating.
