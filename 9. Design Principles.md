# Design Principles

| No | Design Principle | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| DP-001 | The motor should be aligned with the arm axis as much as possible. | To reduce the bending moment |
| DP-002 | Sharp internal corners should be avoided. | Stress concentration |
| DP-003 | Unnecessary material usage should be avoided. | Weight optimization |
| DP-004 | Drilling the carbon fiber arm should be avoided as much as possible. | Benchmark observations |
| DP-005 | The motor mount must provide sufficient rigidity. | Benchmark |
